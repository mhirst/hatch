Hatch — local-first app sharing for teams.

This is an early prototype. By installing Hatch you acknowledge:

- Hatch runs a local daemon (hatchd) on your machine.
- It builds Docker containers for the app folders you ask it to deploy.
- It uses Tailscale to make those containers reachable to teammates you
  explicitly grant access to.
- Hatch stores app names, container IDs, and access grants in a local SQLite
  database under your user data folder. Your reporting data never leaves your
  laptop.
- Source code is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.

Hatch does not collect telemetry. Logs stay on your machine.

You may terminate use at any time by quitting the app and removing the
installation folder; the local data folder can be removed separately if
desired.
